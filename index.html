01 / ABOUT

A career spent putting infrastructure underneath things that matter.

I'm an engineering leader who spends most of his time on the line between platform infrastructure and the products that sit on top of it. The work has two halves: the systems half (Kubernetes at scale, cloud-native, data platforms, AI infrastructure) and the people half (organisations of a hundred-plus engineers, P&L, hiring, conviction during the messy years of a platform).

Today, at NetApp, I'm leading the AI Data Engine: the layer that lets Fortune 500 finance, healthcare, and government teams put their own data behind production RAG and LLM applications. Before that, eight years at Salesforce, scaling Kubernetes from a private-datacenter experiment to the platform that runs Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, Tableau, Slack, MuleSoft, Einstein, and Data Cloud.

I write, I speak, I file patents when there's something worth filing. Most of the time I'm just shipping.
